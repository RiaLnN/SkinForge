SkinForge __VERSION__ (__PLATFORM__)
=====================================

Mix and customize osu!lazer and osu!stable skins with a live interactive preview and universal .osk export.

1. Start the app:
   - GUI: run skinforge (or skinforge.exe on Windows)
   - CLI: run skinforge-cli --help
2. The app automatically detects your osu!lazer and osu!stable installations and loads all installed skins with LAZER / STABLE badges.
3. Select a base skin, choose donor elements across 42 slots in 15 sub-groups (Cursor, Gameplay, HUD & Playfield, Menus & Screens, Audio), and customize combo colours.
4. Test your mix on the interactive 60 fps playfield preview (click the hitcircle to trigger 300/100/50/Miss judgements) and audition sounds with Play/Stop controls.
5. Choose whether to auto-open the exported file in osu!lazer, osu!stable (uses Wine automatically on Linux), or Save only, and click "Export .osk Skin".

Keep the "bridge" folder next to the executable, it is required to read osu!lazer's Realm database.
No .NET or Rust installation is needed for pre-built release binaries.

Full documentation: README.md
